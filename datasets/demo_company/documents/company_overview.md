# NovaCloud — Company Overview

NovaCloud is a fictional cloud-native platform that helps small
and mid-sized engineering teams deploy, scale, and observe
containerized workloads without managing their own Kubernetes
clusters. The company was founded in 2018 in a small office
above a coffee shop in Lisbon and has since grown to operate in
four regions: `us-east-1`, `us-west-2`, `eu-west-1`, and
`ap-southeast-1`. As of the most recent internal quarterly
report, NovaCloud employs 312 people across product, platform,
go-to-market, and customer engineering.

## Mission statement

The mission of NovaCloud, as written on the back of the company
napkins in 2018 and later engraved on a plaque in the Lisbon
office, is: "Make infrastructure boring, so engineers can
build interesting things." The mission has been refined several
times. A 2022 rewrite shortened it to "Infrastructure that
disappears." Employees often quote the shorter version when
explaining the product to new hires.

## Leadership

The CEO of NovaCloud is Mariana Alves, who co-founded the
company with her university roommate, Tomáš Dvořák. Tomáš leads
the platform engineering team and reports directly to Mariana.
The head of customer engineering is Priya Raghavan, who joined
in 2021 after eight years at a large enterprise database vendor.
The head of finance is currently a placeholder; that search
was reopened after the previous finance lead left in March 2025.
The board of directors includes Mariana, Tomáš, two
independent VCs, and a rotating observer seat.

## Office locations

NovaCloud maintains three physical offices. The headquarters
is in Lisbon, Portugal, at Rua da Madalena 184. The
Americas office is in Toronto, Canada, at 401 Richmond Street
West, on the 11th floor. The APAC office is in Singapore, at
8 Marina Boulevard, Tower 1. All three offices host customers
events roughly every quarter, and the Lisbon office hosts the
annual company offsite in September.

## Funding history

NovaCloud has raised four rounds of venture funding:

- Seed (2019): €2.4M, led by Atlantic Ventures.
- Series A (2020): €14M, led by Northwind Partners.
- Series B (2022): €48M, led by Helios Capital, with
  participation from all prior investors.
- Series C (2024): €90M, led by Pacific Growth Fund.

The company has not yet announced plans for an IPO. Mariana
has stated publicly that an IPO is "a tool, not a goal."

## Customer base

NovaCloud serves approximately 4,800 paying customers,
ranging from two-person startups to public companies in the
logistics, gaming, and fintech sectors. The largest single
customer accounts for 6.2% of annual recurring revenue; the
top ten customers together account for 31.4%. No customer is
classified as strategic unless their ARR exceeds $500K.

## Cultural notes (for internal use)

The company operates on a four-day workweek during July and
August. Internal Slack channels are archived after 90 days.
All-hands meetings happen on the first Wednesday of every
month and are recorded. The company song, sung at the annual
offsite, is to the tune of "Hey Jude" with custom lyrics.
New employees receive a "Day 1" notebook that lists the names
of everyone they will work with in their first month.
