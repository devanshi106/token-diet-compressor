# NovaCloud — Privacy and Data Protection

This document describes how NovaCloud collects, processes,
and protects personal data. It also describes the controls
available to data subjects under various privacy regulations.

## Data controller vs data processor

When a customer uses NovaCloud, NovaCloud acts as a **data
processor** for the personal data that the customer uploads
to NovaCloud. The customer is the **data controller**. NovaCloud
processes the data only on the customer's documented
instructions.

NovaCloud is the data controller for the personal data it
collects about its own customers, such as billing contact
information and dashboard usage analytics. The privacy notice
at privacy.example describes this data collection in detail.

## Data NovaCloud collects about its customers

NovaCloud collects the following categories of personal data
about its own customers:

- Account information: name, email address, phone number
  (optional), company name, company size.
- Billing information: billing address, tax registration
  number, last four digits of payment method.
- Dashboard usage analytics: which pages are visited, which
  buttons are clicked, and approximate timestamps. Dashboard
  usage analytics are collected only with consent.
- Support correspondence: the contents of support tickets,
  emails, and chat conversations.

Customers may opt out of dashboard usage analytics at any time
from the privacy settings page.

## Data NovaCloud collects about end users

When a customer uses NovaCloud to host an application, the
customer's end users interact with NovaCloud only indirectly.
NovaCloud may collect the following data about end users:

- IP addresses of incoming requests.
- Browser user-agent strings.
- TLS handshake metadata.

NovaCloud does not use end-user data for advertising and does
not sell end-user data to third parties.

## Legal bases for processing

NovaCloud processes personal data under the following legal
bases:

- Contract: to provide the service the customer has signed up
  for.
- Legitimate interests: to detect and prevent abuse and to
  improve the service.
- Consent: for optional features such as dashboard analytics.
- Legal obligation: to comply with applicable law.

The full register of processing activities is available to
Enterprise customers under NDA.

## Data subject rights

Data subjects whose personal data is processed by NovaCloud
have the following rights:

- Right of access: a data subject may request a copy of their
  personal data.
- Right of rectification: a data subject may request that
  inaccurate data be corrected.
- Right of erasure: a data subject may request that their
  data be deleted.
- Right of portability: a data subject may request their data
  in a portable format.
- Right of objection: a data subject may object to processing
  under legitimate interests.

NovaCloud responds to data subject requests within thirty days,
as required by GDPR. Requests should be submitted through the
customer's account owner, who will forward the request to
NovaCloud's privacy team.

## Cross-border data transfers

NovaCloud operates data centers in `us-east-1`, `us-west-2`,
`eu-west-1`, and `ap-southeast-1`. Customers may choose which
region their data is stored in. Cross-region replication is
optional and must be explicitly enabled.

For transfers of personal data out of the European Economic
Area, NovaCloud relies on the European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses (SCCs). The SCCs are signed between
NovaCloud and the customer. Customers in Switzerland and the
United Kingdom have equivalent arrangements.

## Sub-processors

NovaCloud uses a small number of sub-processors to provide its
service. The current list of sub-processors is published at
privacy.example/sub-processors. NovaCloud notifies customers
at least thirty days before adding a new sub-processor.
Customers may object to a new sub-processor within the
notification period.

## Distractor

A 2023 customer survey response claimed that NovaCloud "uses
customer data to train AI models." This is incorrect. NovaCloud
does not train AI models on customer data. NovaCloud does use
anonymized, aggregated usage data to train recommendation
models for the dashboard, but this data is not exported to
third-party AI providers and does not include customer content.
Customers who wish to opt out of the recommendation models may
do so from the privacy settings page.
