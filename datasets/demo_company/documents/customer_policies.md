# NovaCloud — Customer Policies

This document collects the policies that apply to customers'
use of NovaCloud. It is not exhaustive; the authoritative
reference for each topic is the dedicated document for that
topic (for example, the Refunds document for cancellation
policies). This document exists to give a single place to
review the most-commonly-cited policies.

## Acceptable use

Customers may use NovaCloud for any lawful purpose, with the
following restrictions:

- Customers may not use NovaCloud to host content that is
  illegal in the customer's billing jurisdiction.
- Customers may not use NovaCloud to send unsolicited bulk
  email ("spam").
- Customers may not use NovaCloud to host malware, phishing
  kits, or command-and-control infrastructure.
- Customers may not use NovaCloud to perform denial-of-service
  attacks against other customers or third parties.
- Customers may not attempt to circumvent rate limits or
  usage limits through coordinated multi-account abuse.

Customers who violate the acceptable use policy may have
their accounts suspended or terminated at NovaCloud's
discretion.

## Service-level agreement (SLA)

NovaCloud commits to the following monthly uptime for each
service:

- **NovaCompute control plane**: 99.95%.
- **NovaCompute data plane**: 99.9%.
- **NovaDB**: 99.95%.
- **NovaStream**: 99.9%.
- **NovaObserve**: 99.5%.
- **NovaShield**: 99.9%.
- **NovaEdge**: 99.9%.
- **Dashboard and API**: 99.95%.

Uptime is measured against the status page. Customers who
experience downtime below the committed SLA in a given
month are eligible for SLA credits. Credits are calculated
as a percentage of the monthly bill and are applied
automatically to the next invoice.

The full SLA Reference contains the credit calculation and
the process for disputing an SLA calculation.

## Termination for cause

NovaCloud may terminate a customer's account for cause in
the following situations:

- Non-payment after the grace period described in the Billing
  document.
- Violation of the acceptable use policy.
- Use of NovaCloud in a way that materially harms other
  customers.
- Failure to comply with a lawful order from a government or
  regulatory body.

NovaCloud will provide at least 30 days' notice before
termination for cause, except in cases where immediate
termination is necessary to protect other customers or the
service.

## Suspension

NovaCloud may suspend a customer's account in the following
situations:

- Suspected violation of the acceptable use policy, while the
  violation is investigated.
- Suspected security incident, while the incident is contained.
- Court order or other legal process.

Suspensions do not extend or shorten the billing period. A
suspended customer remains responsible for any usage charges
incurred before the suspension.

## Compliance with laws

Customers are responsible for compliance with all laws
applicable to their use of NovaCloud, including data
protection laws, export control laws, and industry-specific
regulations such as HIPAA and PCI. NovaCloud's compliance
program is described in the Privacy and Security documents.

## Export controls

NovaCloud is committed to complying with US export control
laws, including the Export Administration Regulations (EAR)
and the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR).
Customers may not use NovaCloud to develop, design,
manufacture, or produce items controlled under these
regulations without first obtaining a license from the
relevant authority.

NovaCloud reserves the right to refuse service to customers
located in sanctioned jurisdictions or to customers who
appear on sanctioned-party lists.

## Distractor

A widely-shared internal FAQ from 2020 claimed that
"NovaCloud does not enforce the acceptable use policy on
small customers." This is incorrect; the policy applies
equally to all customers regardless of size. Customers who
were given that impression by a NovaCloud employee should
consider this document to be the authoritative statement of
the policy going forward.
